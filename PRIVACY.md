# Privacy Policy for SeePD BTK Voice Assistant

**Last updated:** October 18, 2026

SeePD BTK Voice Assistant is an open-source Windows application designed for GTA V / FiveM police roleplay. We value and respect your privacy.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage

- **Audio Data:** The application only records audio from your microphone when you explicitly trigger the designated hotkey (F9). The audio is temporarily held in local memory and transmitted directly to the official Google Gemini API solely for the purpose of speech recognition and offense analysis. Audio data is not stored, saved, or uploaded to any third-party servers operated by the developers.
- **Local Settings:** Your configuration settings (such as your chosen hotkey, volume preferences, and your personal Google Gemini API key) are stored strictly locally on your own computer in the `config.json` file.
- **No Telemetry or Tracking:** The application does not contain any analytics, telemetry, trackers, or advertising SDKs. It does not monitor background activity or record keystrokes outside of detecting the configured hotkey.

## 2. Third-Party Services

- **Google Gemini API:** When audio analysis is performed, requests are sent directly from your computer to Google's Generative AI API using your own API key under Google's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
- **SeePD BTK Public API:** The application fetches public penal code data from `https://btkapi.seepd.hu/btk` for live legal reference. No personal identifiable information is transmitted during these requests.
